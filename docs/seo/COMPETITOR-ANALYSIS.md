## What they do that we do not

1. **Listicles.** Alice Labs and OSKI publish "best AI consultants in Gothenburg/Sweden" lists and put themselves first. These lists rank and are read by AI search engines. We are in none of them.
2. **Price numbers.** Alice Labs shows "from" prices. We say "fixed price after a call".
3. **Trustpilot and Wikidata in `sameAs`.** Third-party entity signals. We have LinkedIn and GitHub only.
4. **Insights / blog.** Alice Labs and Opsio both publish. We have none.

## What we have that they lack

1. **A named builder.** Rivals sell a team brand. We sell the engineer who writes the code. Strong E-E-A-T.
2. **Live own products** (SökHem, OpenAudit, Wez, Lingo) with case pages.
3. **Local AI on own hardware** (RTX 5090 box). No rival in the results writes about running models on site for GDPR reasons.
4. **Real local focus.** Our city pages already name the local industry (chemistry in Stenungsund, logistics in Ale). Alice Labs' Göteborg page has no local cases.
5. **Fast, clean site.** Static Astro, small JS. Most rivals run heavier stacks.
